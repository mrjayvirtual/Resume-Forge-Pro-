# Brand system

Both products belong to the JayVirtual product family. Keep their product identities distinct while sharing a premium visual language.

## Shared palette
- Midnight: #080D19
- Panel: #141A2B
- Violet: #9B8CFF
- Teal: #63D9D0
- Warm accent: #E9D5A1
- Primary text: #F3F5FC
- Muted text: #9AA7C4

## Logo use
- Use `public/brand/logo.svg` for the horizontal lockup.
- Use `public/brand/favicon.svg` for browser tabs and compact app surfaces.
- Preserve the original aspect ratio; never stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of its outer icon stroke.
- Keep the product name legible; do not replace it with the parent brand.

## Product distinction
- Rubric Gym: evaluation, practice, rubric literacy, and measurable improvement. The shield/grid mark communicates structured assessment and trust.
- Resume Forge Pro: career documents, clarity, and professional progress. The document/letter mark communicates a refined career toolkit.
